# Course Foundations

This section includes information about the structure and goals of the course. It may not be kept up to date throughout the semester, but is intended to provide information for future instructors about what the course should include, based on the design process conducted in AY 25-26.

These documents were developed through open planning in the
[CU-Computational-Methods-Planning](https://github.com/zsunberg/CU-Computational-Methods-Planning)
repository and are now maintained here.
