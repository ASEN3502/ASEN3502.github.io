# Learning Resources

Need help? Confused about something? If you feel behind, our goal is to help you catch up! Here are some resources to help:

## Start with the course staff

1. **Ask on Slack.** Post in the appropriate channel. Questions asked there help
   everyone. See the [Canvas announcement](https://canvas.colorado.edu/courses/143236/discussion_topics/1949808)
   for the invite link.
2. **Come to Aerospace Study Hall.** We plan to have at least one TA at each aerospace study hall. You can [join the study hall canvas course here](https://canvas.colorado.edu/enroll/R7KLTH).
3. **Come to office hours.** Dr. Sunberg's office hours are on the [Teaching Team](teaching-team) page.

If you are having difficulty connecting through these options, please let us know on Slack!

## MATLAB

- [MATLAB Tutorials from MathWorks](https://www.mathworks.com/support/learn-with-matlab-tutorials.html) -- self-paced, interactive courses. *MATLAB Onramp* is the best starting point if you want to shore up the basics.
- Chapters 2 and 3 from the textbook cover Matlab programming.
- CU Boulder students get [MATLAB for free through the campus](https://oit.colorado.edu/software-hardware/software-catalog/matlab).

## Campus support

- [Engineering Academic Support and Tutoring](https://www.colorado.edu/engineering-advising/academic-support-tutoring)
  -- free drop-in tutoring at the BOLD Student Success Center, Engineering
  Fellows, and course-specific help.
- [Academic Coaching](https://www.colorado.edu/engineering-advising/academiccoaching)
  -- free one-on-one help with study habits, time management, and planning.
- [Counseling and Psychiatric Services (CAPS)](https://www.colorado.edu/counseling/)
  -- free and confidential mental health support.
- [Disability Services](https://www.colorado.edu/disabilityservices/) --
  accommodations for disabilities and temporary medical conditions.
